# Figure previews

The four plates are preserved from the [six-page report](../reports/positive.pdf). Panel letters and full captions are in that report. The current standalone PDFs for Figures 2–4 are linked below. The historical release also contains supporting tables.

1. **Design and encoding.** Count targets, shared reference context, candidate branches, within-token position and observed token geometry. [Description](overview.description.txt).

   ![Design and encoding](overview.png)

2. **Predictive evidence and calibration.** Full-population density plots, window and held-out-chromosome partition schematics, independent assay-scale calibration, and the measured NLL cost of omitting it. [PDF](Figure_2.pdf) · [Description](prediction.description.txt).

   ![Predictive evidence](prediction.png)

3. **Sequence patterns and seqlet example.** Focal-seqlet coverage with explicit LateBranch and Vanilla legends, a measured CTCF substitution-response example with its archived seqlet, and ATCG pattern logos from the historical balanced interpretation cohort. [PDF](Figure_3.pdf) · [Description](patterns.description.txt).

   ![Sequence patterns](patterns.png)

4. **Joint TF-MoDISco and attention.** A CTCF case with a track legend, the rationale and settings for the joint per-assay TF-MoDISco call, plus population attention-distance distributions. [PDF](Figure_4.pdf) · [Description](attention.description.txt).

   ![Pattern and attention alignment](attention.png)

The pattern and attention plates describe an earlier model and cohort. They are not causal evidence for the position gate in the released count-only checkpoint.
